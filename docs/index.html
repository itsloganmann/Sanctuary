Core Features
Built for critical moments
Every feature is designed to work when it matters most, with minimal friction and maximum reliability.
⚠

Panic Button

Hold-to-activate mechanism prevents false triggers. Dispatches your GPS coordinates to all trusted contacts via SMS within 2 seconds.

⏱

Dead Man's Switch

Configurable check-in timer automatically escalates to an alert if you fail to check in. Customizable intervals from 15 minutes to 24 hours.

📍

Live Location

Real-time GPS tracking using iOS CoreLocation with CLBackgroundActivitySession, delivering continuous updates even when the app is backgrounded.

♥

Consent Manager

Tinder-style swipe interface for expressing and documenting personal boundaries across 8 customizable categories. QR code partner linking.

🔐

Lock Screen Widget

Interactive WidgetKit extension enables check-in and panic activation without unlocking your phone. Dynamic Island support included.

🔔

Smart Alerts

Supabase Realtime powers instant push notifications and live activity updates. Trusted contacts receive structured SMS with location links.

How It Works
Designed for speed and simplicity
From installation to active protection in under 3 minutes.
01

Download and Sign Up

Install from the App Store. Verify your phone number with a one-time SMS passcode. Your number becomes your secure account identifier.

02

Add Trusted Contacts

Designate up to 10 trusted contacts who will receive your location and status during a safety event. They receive a brief orientation SMS.

03

Configure Your Safety Plan

Set check-in intervals, customize your emergency message, and pair with a partner via QR code for consent management features.

04

Protected 24/7

Add the widget to your lock screen. One tap activates monitoring. Hold SOS in a panic. Your contacts are alerted immediately.
